Where the verdict stands

Static evidence is complete and clean. All eight S2 findings and all nine S3 findings that are code-checkable now measure zero, and each is enforced by a CI gate rather than by discipline. The runtime half is not. The workbench’s light/dark walk, keyboard pass, screen-reader pass, zoom and device checks were never performed — they need a browser. Until they are, every runtime unit reads blank, and a blank unit is not a pass.

Two S3 items are honestly partial: the shadow and padding scales in L2, and adoption of the Card/Badge/EmptyState/loading primitives in L4 and L5. The primitives exist and are tested; pages still hand-roll many of them. One S4 (L7, dead components) is open by decision.

06 · Rules

How the verdict is computed

The workbench applies these rules in order and stops at the first that matches. Nothing here is a judgement call.

#RuleCurrent state
1Any S1 open → Rejectno S1 exists
2Any unit not reviewed → Incompleteruntime units blank — this is the live rule
3An unrated failure → Triagenot reached
4Any S2 open → Reject0 of 12 open
5Only S3/S4 open → Approve with fixesthis is where it lands once rule 2 clears
6Nothing open → Approvenot yet

What to expect. Complete the runtime passes and rule 2 clears. Rule 4 is already satisfied — no S2 is open, and each is CI-enforced so none can silently return. That lands the verdict at Approve with fixes under rule 5, because L2, L4, L5 are partial and L7 is open by decision. Reaching a clean Approve means finishing shadow/padding adoption, adopting the surface primitives across pages, and deciding L7 — all of which are follow-up work, not defects in what shipped.
