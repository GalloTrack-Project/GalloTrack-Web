Executive Summary & Test Orientation

Milestone 1: Prototype V1 (MVP)

This document serves as the formal User Acceptance Test (UAT) and Feedback Instrument for the Microplastic AI Prototype Version 1 shipped by Syntaxure Labs. The primary goal of Version 1 is to validate the real-world bench ergonomics, camera resolution, specimen capture flow, verification UX, and database tracking on your actual smartphones and clip-on microscope lenses before moving to final automated model weights.

📅 Shipped: Oct 2, 2026 📱 PWA Mobile Prototype ⚙️ Deterministic Mock Classifier (v1 UI Engine) 🧪 Lab Pre-Test Ready 🏆 Scilympics Ready Target: Oct 18–20

⚡ Important Research Update Alignment

Lab-based Microplastic Pre-Test: Per Adviser Roberto Yleana's advice from your proposal defense, your team will first benchmark the system using known laboratory-standard microplastics before testing barangay canal samples. Version 1 supports this immediately: use the Replicate and "Test Photo" toggles to document your lab-standard tests cleanly!
Scilympics Acceleration: To help qualify and compete by October 18–20, quick feedback on this form allows us to finalize any UI tweaks while the custom AI model finishes training on your newly uploaded dataset.

Scope Governance

What is Included & What to Expect in Version 1

To ensure a clear and fair acceptance test, please review what is functioning in this prototype versus what is slated for the upcoming Model Version 1 release.

Included in Version 1 (Testable Today)

Active
  • Installable Mobile Web App (PWA) Installs cleanly to Android / iPhone home screen; opens in fullscreen without browser url bars.
  • Study Hierarchy & Sample Code Generation All 4 Oton study locations (PS, PE, PW, PN); auto-codes sample bottles (e.g. OTON-PS-R1); rejects duplicate replicates with clear alerts.
  • Lab-Tailored Camera Viewport Dark camera background with circular dashed guide ring to align your clip-on microscope lens; gallery upload & file-picker fallback for all phone models.
  • Interactive Verification Workflow Display of predicted morphological type (Fiber, Fragment, Film, Foam, Pellet) & confidence meter; dual "Confirm" vs "Correct" buttons that preserve both AI guess and researcher truth.
  • Specimen History & Search Filters Filter records by location, replicate, verification status, and test-only photos; search by IMG- ID or date.
  • Analytics & Automated Model Check Matrix Visual type distribution bars, confusion matrix calculator, precision/recall/F1 metrics, and full CSV raw data export.

Not Included in Version 1 (Planned for Model V1)

Excluded / Next
  • Custom Trained Roboflow AI Weights V1 uses a deterministic mock engine (SHA-256 simulation) to validate UI loading states and math. Real trained weights will be plugged in once your newly uploaded dataset completes training.
  • User Authentication & Logins Intentionally open prototype to eliminate login/password friction during lab bench handoffs among group members.
  • Delete Functions Permanent deletion of samples/photos is intentionally disabled to preserve research audit trails and data integrity.
  • Chemical / Polymer Testing (FTIR/Raman) App only analyzes particle shape (morphology) from micrographs, not chemical composition.
  • Automated Thesis Chapter Writing App provides raw CSV and metric calculations; statistical interpretation and thesis manuscript remain with the research team.
